# mCaffeine Brand Strategy

## Independent Brand Strategy Project

A social media content marketing and brand strategy project focused on mCaffeine.

## Project Overview

This project explores how mCaffeine can strengthen consumer engagement by connecting social media attention with repeat purchase behaviour.

The strategy focuses on:

- Social media content
- Consumer engagement
- Brand outreach
- Competitor positioning
- Consumer journey
- Social-commerce
- Repeat purchase

## Strategic Idea

### The mCaffeine Reset

The project proposes a **30-day social-commerce experiment** designed to move consumers through:

**DISCOVER → TRY → REPEAT**

The objective is to turn mCaffeine from a product-led brand into a repeatable self-care ritual.

## Consumer Journey

**Discover**  
Reels, creators, products and community content generate awareness and curiosity.

**Try**  
Product and routine content gives consumers a reason to act.

**Repeat**  
Reminders, offers and UGC encourage customers to return.

## Competitive Analysis

The project compares mCaffeine with brands including:

- Dot & Key
- Minimalist
- The Derma Co
- Plum
- WishCare

The strategy identifies an opportunity for mCaffeine to strengthen its **lifestyle + experience** positioning and own the idea of a **reset ritual**.

## Content Strategy

The content approach connects:

**CONTENT → CONVERSATION → COMMERCE → RETURN**

Every campaign asset is designed to carry a clear next action.

## Brand Outreach & Engagement

The project includes proof of:

- LinkedIn content and posts
- Social media creative development
- Brand outreach
- Email communication
- Slide deck sharing
- Consumer-facing campaign concepts

## Key Strategic Insight

The goal is not one viral post.

The goal is to build a **repeatable purchase loop**:

**Reach → Engagement → Conversion → Repeat**
